# Q2 Earnings Call Transcript Excerpt — Project Alder / TechCorp

**Date**: July 24, 2026
**Participants**: Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Lead Equity Analysts

## Management Prepared Remarks

**CEO**: Thank you everyone for joining. Q2 was a pivotal quarter. Revenue reached $124.5M, representing a 15% year-over-year increase. Gross margin expanded to 44.5%, up 120 basis points sequentially.

**CFO**: Based on strong operational momentum, we are raising our full-year FY26 revenue guidance to $495.0M–$505.0M (up from $480.0M–$490.0M previously). Adjusted EBITDA margin for the full year is projected at 18.2%.

## Analyst Q&A

**Analyst (Morgan Stanley)**: Could you elaborate on the margin trajectory given supply chain pressure in APAC?
**CFO**: We expect mild gross margin fluctuation in Q3 due to component lead times, but annual targets remain fully intact.
