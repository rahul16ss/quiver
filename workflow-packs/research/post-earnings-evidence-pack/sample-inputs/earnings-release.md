# AeroTech Systems Reports Q2 Financial Results

**Date**: July 28, 2026  
**Ticker**: ATS (Synthetic)

## Financial Highlights

- **Total Revenue**: $124.5 million, up 18.4% year-over-year compared to $105.1 million in Q2 2025.
- **Gross Margin**: 44.5%, an expansion of 180 basis points year-over-year.
- **Net Income**: $18.2 million, compared to $14.1 million in Q2 2025.
- **Diluted EPS**: $1.42, compared to $1.10 in the prior-year period.
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: $142.0 million, up 21.2% year-over-year.
- **Operating Cash Flow**: $24.8 million for the quarter.

## Business Commentary

AeroTech Systems achieved strong top-line momentum driven by strategic expansion in commercial aerospace software and multi-year enterprise renewals. Net retention rate remained healthy at 114%.

## Guidance Update

Full-year 2026 revenue guidance is raised to $495.0 million – $505.0 million (previously $485.0M – $495.0M). Full-year operating margin guidance is reaffirmed at 21.5%.
