# AeroTech Systems Q2 Earnings Call Transcript Excerpt

**Management Attendees**:
- CEO: Marcus Vance
- CFO: Elena Rostova

## Key Transcript Quotes

**Marcus Vance (CEO)**:
> "Our enterprise expansion strategy delivered exceptional results in Q2. ARR reached $142.0 million, supported by 114% net retention. We saw particular strength in software subscriptions, which grew 24% year-over-year."

**Elena Rostova (CFO)**:
> "Gross margin reached 44.5%, benefiting from favorable product mix and operational efficiencies. We are raising our full-year revenue outlook to $495.0M – $505.0M. Operating cash flow reached $24.8 million."

**Analyst Question (Morgan Stanley)**:
> "Elena, could you unpack the gross margin progression for H2? Are there any supply chain headwinds to watch?"

**Elena Rostova (CFO)**:
> "We expect gross margin to fluctuate slightly in Q3 due to planned infrastructure investments, but we remain fully on track for our 21.5% full-year operating margin target. Supply chain risks are managed through multi-vendor sourcing agreements."
