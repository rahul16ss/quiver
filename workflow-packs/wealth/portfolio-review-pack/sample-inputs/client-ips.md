# Client Investment Policy Statement (IPS) Constraints

**Account Name**: Meridian Family Trust  
**Risk Profile**: Moderate Growth & Income  

## Investment Mandate & Asset Allocation Limits

- **Public Equity Ceiling**: Maximum 70.0% of portfolio total value.
- **Fixed Income Target**: Minimum 30.0% allocation (includes High Yield up to 10.0%).
- **Single Equity Exposure Cap**: No individual equity holding may exceed 6.0% of portfolio value.
- **Target Portfolio Yield**: Minimum 3.2% annual dividend/interest yield.
- **ESG Quality Constraint**: All equity & corporate bond holdings must maintain an ESG rating of BBB or higher.

## Review Frequency

Quarterly formal portfolio review required. Any IPS breach must be surfaced in the reviewer checklist for advisor sign-off.
