Welcome to Quiver

Quiver turns your files, research, and review rules into native Office documents with sources you can inspect. Your memory and documents are stored as files on your computer; you choose where prompts are sent.

01 Your memoryHuman-readable and reviewable
02 Your contextVisible before Quiver runs
03 Your sign-offDrafts stay reviewable

Stored in your computer's keychain or credential manager when available. Quiver uses a local fallback only when that store cannot be reached.

In Settings, set the model name and provider address (project folder stays on your machine) to point Quiver at any OpenAI-compatible service.

i The app runs on this computer (loopback only, never on the network). If your provider is remote, approved prompts and tool results may leave the machine; Quiver will block calls that violate the engagement's sensitivity rules.

Prefer to add your model key later? Just close this window and set it in Settings. Approval settings control what Quiver can do without asking (full auto is opt-in).
